MinerSweeper

MinerSweeper is an application designed to discover and identify relevant processes in your organization through the use of metaheuristic optimization techniques applied to the most widely used mining algorithms.

Main Features

  • Discovery: Start the process discovery procedure.
  • Import: Import execution configurations shared by colleagues or from past runs.
  • History: View a detailed record of previous executions and their solutions.
  • Logs: Upload new event logs and manage those already present in the application.

For more details on how to use the application, visit the Information section.
